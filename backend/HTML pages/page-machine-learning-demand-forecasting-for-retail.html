What is ML demand forecasting for retail?

Definition

Machine learning demand forecasting for retail is an AI system that combines time-series models with store-level transaction data, weather, and promotional calendars to predict unit demand at the individual SKU and location level. By grounding every forecast in historical sell-through rates and real-time inventory feeds, it eliminates guesswork, reduces stockout penalties by up to 35%, and automates replenishment decisions with measurable ROI.

Forecasting boundaries

When ML forecasting is not the right choice

Forecasting has its limits

Demand forecasting is designed for patterns with sufficient historical data and recognizable seasonality. It is not suited for one-time product launches or chaotic supply chain disruptions where human judgment and buffer stock are essential. Any deployment must patch in human override for promotional blips and unforeseen demand shifts.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do you ensure forecast accuracy at the SKU level?
We use a hybrid model combining statistical seasonality decomposition (Prophet) and gradient-boosted trees for residual learning. Cross-validation and backtesting against holdout periods ensure reliability. Continuous drift monitoring triggers retraining when accuracy drops below thresholds.
How do you handle seasonal and promotional spikes?
Seasonal features are explicitly engineered using Fourier terms and holiday indicators. Promotional events are modeled as external regressors with decay effects. The model learns price elasticity and markdown sensitivity from historical promotion cycles.
Can you quantify stockout cost for ROI justification?
We calculate stockout cost using lost contribution margin plus expedited fulfillment expenses. By backtesting historical out-of-stocks against missed sales, we project potential revenue recovered through improved forecast accuracy. This provides a clear ROI for the investment.
Do I need my own data science team to use this?
No. I handle the entire pipeline from data ingestion to model deployment. You just need access to your historical demand data and inventory records. I provide documentation and training for your in-house team to interpret forecasts and trigger actions.
